Step 2 — Open Gmail signature settings

  1. Open Gmail on your computer (this doesn't work on mobile).
  2. Click the gear icon (top right) → See all settings.
  3. Scroll down to the Signature section.
  4. If you already have a signature, click it to edit. Otherwise click + Create new and name it "Show Limousines".

Step 3 — Paste and save

  1. Click inside the signature editor box to place your cursor.
  2. Delete anything already there (select all with Ctrl + A, press Delete).
  3. Paste with Ctrl + V.
  4. You should see the logo, your details, and the Facebook / Instagram buttons appear.
  5. Under Signature defaults, set both "For new emails" and "On reply/forward" to this signature.
  6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

To test it, compose a new email and check the signature appears with the logo and social icons visible. If anything looks off, let Andy know and we'll sort it.
